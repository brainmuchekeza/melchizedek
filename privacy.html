Privacy Notice

This website is designed to help customers contact Melchizedek Technologies and request quotations for technology, connectivity, power and security services.

Information you provide

When you use our quotation form, you may provide your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, location, service requirements, property type and other project details.

How information is used

Information supplied through the quotation form is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and communicate about the requested service.

How this website handles enquiries

The quotation form currently prepares an enquiry message in your browser and opens WhatsApp for you to send it. This static website does not use a built-in server database to store quotation submissions.

Third-party services

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp according to its own policies. You should review the privacy information of services you use to communicate with us.
