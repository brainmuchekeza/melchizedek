OUR STORY

Technology should solve real problems.

Melchizedek Technologies was built around a simple idea: reliable technology should not be limited by location. From internet connectivity and solar power to CCTV and security systems, we help customers get practical solutions installed where they need them.

Our work has grown through hands-on installations for schools, businesses, homes and institutions. We understand that every site is different, so we focus on solutions that are practical, properly installed and easy for customers to use.

“Connect. Power. Secure.”

That is more than a slogan. It represents the three areas at the heart of what we do: bringing people online, helping properties access dependable power, and protecting the places and assets that matter.
