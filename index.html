FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions before you request a quotation.

Do you install Starlink across Zimbabwe?+

Yes. Melchizedek Technologies provides Starlink installation services nationwide, including towns and rural communities.

Can you provide solar systems for schools and businesses?+

Yes. We design and install solar solutions for homes, schools, businesses and institutions. The system is sized around your power requirements.

Do you install CCTV systems?+

Yes. We supply and professionally install CCTV systems, including recording and camera configuration. Night-time viewing can be supported in black and white or colour depending on the selected camera technology.

Can I combine Starlink, solar and CCTV in one project?+

Absolutely. We can coordinate multiple solutions so connectivity, backup power, surveillance and networking work together.

Do you offer custom quotations?+

Yes. Starting prices are useful for orientation, but larger or specialised projects are quoted according to site requirements, equipment and installation scope.

How do I request a quotation?+

Use our online quote form. Your completed enquiry is prepared as a WhatsApp message to our team for follow-up.
