FAQ

Common questions.

Can CCTV see at night?

Suitable night-vision cameras can record at night. Infrared systems commonly switch to black-and-white footage in low light.

Can I view the cameras from my phone?

Many supported systems can be configured for remote viewing, subject to internet and system compatibility.

Do you install CCTV nationwide?

Yes. We install CCTV for schools, homes, businesses and institutions nationwide.
